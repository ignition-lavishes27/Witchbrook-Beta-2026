## 🕹️ About

Witchbrook is a cozy life-sim and social RPG set in Mossport, a vibrant seaside town where aspiring witches attend Witchbrook College. Learn magical abilities, build friendships and romance, explore the surrounding world, customize your cottage, and shape your life on the path to graduation. The game is planned for 2026, and this beta test gives selected players the opportunity to help shape the experience while receiving a free game key.

Witchbrook is for players who enjoy cozy life sims, social RPGs, magical school settings, exploration, customization, and relaxed cooperative play.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🎓 **Witchcraft College** — Train as a witch and learn new magical abilities at Witchbrook College.
- 🌊 **Explore Mossport** — Discover a lively seaside town filled with characters, secrets, events, and activities.
- 💞 **Friendships and Romance** — Build meaningful relationships and find romance among a colorful cast of characters.
- 🌿 **Cottage Customization** — Renovate your woodland cottage and transform its surrounding garden.
- 🧺 **Open-Ended Activities** — Spend your time foraging, crafting, visiting markets, attending events, and more.
- 🤝 **Cooperative Play** — Share the magical life-sim experience with up to four players.

**A:** Simply sign in with your Steam account, browse available beta programs, request access to games you're interested in, and start testing. After you complete the beta, you'll receive a free Steam key for the full game upon its official release.

**Q: Do I need to pay to join?**

**Q: When will I receive the Steam key?**

**Q: Will I get access right away?**

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
